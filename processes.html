S01Opportunity intake and channel handoffCreation · Stage 02 open questions

A single traceable opportunity reaches the right seller with its originating Contact and intake context.

When this starts

An inbound, BDR, partner, agentic or seller-generated buying signal needs a sales response.

Suggested operating lead

Marketing Ops for intake; Sales Ops for routing

Confirm named ownership/backup in Q41. Approved role authority still applies.

Connected teams

BDR leadership, Partner Ops, AE, MAP owner

Approved foundation

  • Marketing, BDR and partner intake uses Stage 0; new Demand Gen opportunity intake is retired in the target design.
  • SAL is recorded at opportunity creation. MQL and AQL are separate source qualification paths; seller-direct entry follows its documented exception.

Input quality to inspect

  • Correct Account and actual originating Contact; source and matching campaign/partner/BDR reference.
  • Customer need and intake evidence; distinguish an introduction, a held meeting and genuine qualification.

Draft operating procedure

  1. Review the incoming signal, check existing opportunities and identify the relevant Account/Contact before creating another deal.
  2. Use the documented Stage 0 path or valid seller-direct Stage 1 path; preserve source event IDs and original qualification context.
  3. Assign the seller and next customer action; make the handoff visible to the receiving team.
  4. Reconcile intake with MAP/partner/BDR records so a failed sync, duplicate or orphan SAL does not disappear from view.

When to inspect

Proposed: inspect intake and unmatched/failed handoffs each business day. Reconcile channel volumes during the confirmed fortnightly pipeline review.

Exception and recovery route

The intake lead retains ownership of an unaccepted handoff; proposed escalation to Sales Ops/BDR or Partner Ops for correction rather than silently creating duplicate records.

Completion and receiving handoff

The assigned AE receives an accessible record, source evidence, Contact and a specific next action; S03 tracks acceptance.

Measures to use

  • Unassigned intake count and oldest age
  • Intake-to-assignment time by channel
  • Duplicate and unmatched-contact rate

Suggested measures; targets, report owners and exact definitions remain to agree in Q44–Q45.

S02Account, Contact and coverage qualityCreation · Stages 0–12 open questions

The team can identify the actual buyer, known end customers and the people entitled to work the deal.

When this starts

A new record, ownership change, new partner or ambiguous commercial chain changes who the deal represents.

Suggested operating lead

Sales Ops for customer/account coverage; Partner Ops for partner coverage

Confirm named ownership/backup in Q41. Approved role authority still applies.

Connected teams

AE, Marketing Ops, Salesforce administration

Approved foundation

  • Account ownership, Opportunity ownership, territory and deal-team participation are separate.
  • An NCP/provider can be the contracting buyer; known underlying customers are associated separately. Account Tier uses the agreed criteria.

Input quality to inspect

  • Legal/customer identity and existing Account/Contact matches; actual contact details.
  • Territory and named-account coverage; partner account manager/technical lead and applicable classification.

Draft operating procedure

  1. Resolve likely duplicates before creating or merging records; preserve links and history through an authorized correction.
  2. Validate the buying entity, expected bill-to and known downstream customer rather than using an end-customer assumption.
  3. Confirm owner, territory, tier and technical/partner coverage using the approved roster.
  4. Check that the receiving AE/SE/partner overlay can see the required records and Files, with the correct commercial and technical edit rights.

When to inspect

Inspect at creation and coverage changes. Proposed: Sales Ops reviews unresolved identity/access exceptions daily; review material coverage gaps fortnightly with pipeline.

Exception and recovery route

Send ambiguous entity/territory cases to the relevant operations owner; record the correction owner and next action. Do not grant broad access to work around a record mismatch.

Completion and receiving handoff

S03 receives an opportunity with a responsible seller; W04 receives the correct participant chain and roles.

Measures to use

  • Duplicates awaiting resolution
  • Owner/territory/contact exceptions
  • Time to restore the correct coverage

S03SAL acceptance, return and routing recoveryStage 0 → qualified pipeline2 open questions

Every handoff has an explicit seller disposition and a managed next step.

When this starts

Suggested operating lead

Assigned AE for the decision; Sales Ops for queue health

Confirm named ownership/backup in Q41. Approved role authority still applies.

Connected teams

Sales leader, BDR/Marketing/Partner originator

Approved foundation

  • Creating SAL is distinct from the seller accepting the handoff.
  • Inbound qualification and assignment gates must pass before qualified entry; preserve acceptance/rejection history.

Input quality to inspect

  • Assigned seller and originating Contact; reason/context for the handoff.
  • Existing SAL decision, actor/time and any prior rejection or reassignment.

Draft operating procedure

  1. Ask the assigned AE to accept or reject using the existing decision process; inspect whether the decision reflects a real review.
  2. For accepted work, agree the next customer conversation and confirm qualification work remains owned.
  3. For a returned/rejected handoff, capture the reason and route the next action to the originating team or approved disposition.
  4. Surface unassigned, undecided and repeatedly returned SALs to the relevant manager with age and an accountable owner.

When to inspect

Proposed: inspect pending and returned SALs daily. Sales leaders inspect their team’s overdue actions in the confirmed weekly team review.

Exception and recovery route

Use an agreed backup when the AE is absent. Reassignment preserves source and previous decisions; it must not create a fresh conversion event just to clear aging.

Completion and receiving handoff

S04 receives accepted, owned work; the originator receives a reason and next action when the handoff cannot progress.

Measures to use

  • Pending SAL age
  • Acceptance/rejection rates with reasons
  • Returned handoffs lacking an owner

Input quality to inspect

Approved foundation

Input quality to inspect

When to inspect

Inspect at intake, a material partner/source change and before booking. The partner group reviews participation and credit exceptions weekly.

Do not let registration, bill-to changes or ownership transfer silently overwrite accepted source. Escalate disputed credit to the named authorized source/Partner Ops owner.

Completion and receiving handoff

Suggested operating lead

Confirm named ownership/backup in Q41. Approved role authority still applies.

Connected teams

SE, sales specialists, Sales Ops

Approved foundation

  • Technical Decision Maker must be an actual Salesforce Contact/role before exiting Stage 3; Decision Maker is required before exiting Stage 4.
  • Use the consolidated MEDDPICC view and existing fields/Contact Roles; avoid duplicate narrative records.

Input quality to inspect

  • Customer pain, decision criteria, Champion, Economic Buyer, decision and paper processes.
  • Technical/Operational/Business Value with baseline, target and source; linked decision-maker Contacts.

Inspect on material customer/stakeholder changes and stage exits. Use weekly team calls for active gaps and fortnightly pipeline reviews for evidence and deal health.

A narrative name does not satisfy a Contact gate. Stale or unverified evidence becomes an owned action; it is not repaired by an invented Contact or unsupported value.

Completion and receiving handoff

W02 receives customer criteria and success measures; C02/C03 receive a supported value case and real decision/paper processes.

W02Technical evaluation and POC governanceStages 2–32 open questions

The team proves the selected solution and customer preference without confusing approval with completion.

When this starts

A technical evaluation or POC is proposed, changes scope or approaches its completion gate.

Suggested operating lead

Assigned SE for evaluation; AE/SE managers for POC approval

Confirm named ownership/backup in Q41. Approved role authority still applies.

Connected teams

AE, customer technical lead, product specialists

Approved foundation

  • POC Approved requires both AE Manager and SE Manager approval. Completion/cancellation is evidenced separately in open-ended POC Completion Evidence.
  • A required POC needs Completed or Cancelled evidence before progression beyond Stage 2. Technical Win requires customer-confirmed preference, not just technical fit.

Input quality to inspect

  • Evaluation purpose, customer criteria, owner, scope and success measures.
  • Both manager decisions; outcome evidence, selected configuration and customer preference.

Draft operating procedure

  1. Agree why the evaluation is needed and how the customer will judge success before committing resources.
  2. Route POC approval to both managers and retain the decisions; record scope changes rather than treating an old approval as blanket authority.
  3. Maintain the evaluation plan, results, blockers and SE next action; escalate a stalled evaluation to the responsible SE/sales leader.
  4. Document Completed/Cancelled evidence and separately confirm Technical Win against customer decision criteria before the applicable progression.

When to inspect

Inspect when a POC is requested/changed, at both manager decisions and before the Stage 2/3 exits. Track unresolved evaluations in weekly team review.

Exception and recovery route

Do not self-check approval, treat one approval as sufficient or equate a successful test with customer preference. Failed gates retain the prior stage.

Completion and receiving handoff

C02 receives a technically supported configuration and approval evidence; G01 receives relevant testing/acceptance context after booking.

Measures to use

  • Approval turnaround
  • Evaluations past their agreed milestone
  • Completion-evidence and Technical Win quality
