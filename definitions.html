Account Lifecycle Stage (ALS)

The account’s current position in the customer lifecycle.

Account Tier

The account-level segmentation choice: Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3. Apply the agreed tier criteria rather than inferring a tier from a single opportunity amount.

Account tier and coverage

Account Tier classifies an account as Tier 1, Tier 2 or Tier 3. Partner coverage and the partner’s admin contact live on the Account; RevOps Notes provides restricted operational context.

Additional Use Case Information

The details of the customer’s workload, context and intended outcome behind the selected Use Case.

Example “Researchers need faster dataset preparation to reduce idle time between model-training experiments.”

Administrative outcomes

Dispositions for records such as duplicates and internal or reference transactions, separate from sales stages and Closed lost.

AE Next Steps

The read-only dated history of changes to the editable Next Step. Existing automation maintains the log; a weekly review is recorded separately.

AI factory

An integrated environment of compute, networking, data infrastructure and software for producing AI models and delivering AI services at scale.

AI training

The process of learning model parameters from data. Infrastructure needs depend on the model, dataset, training method and scale.

Amount

The monetary value of the opportunity. Once a Primary Quote is selected, it supplies the quoted value.

AQL — Agentic Qualified Lead

The Contact qualification milestone supplied by PAX.AI.

Associated accounts

The organizations involved in an opportunity, with their roles, impact, contacts and engagement plans.

Association Engagement Status

The Active/Inactive state of an associated account’s engagement in the opportunity.

Association Sales Contact

The sales contact for the account associated with a deal, distinct from its technical contact.

Association Technical Contact

The technical contact for the account associated with a deal, distinct from its sales contact.

Booking confirmation

Order Management’s confirmation that the order is reconciled and booked. It establishes Closed won and locks booked facts and Close Date.

Business Value

The quantified economic justification for the purchase, based on customer-validated benefits, costs and assumptions.

Example “Avoid $120,000 in annual operating costs against an $80,000 annual solution cost, using customer-validated assumptions.”

Buying committee

The people involved in evaluating, influencing, funding and approving the purchase, linked as Salesforce Contacts with their opportunity roles.

Champion

An internal customer advocate whose influence and support are demonstrated through actions that help the opportunity progress.

Example The architect arranges access to the budget owner and helps validate the internal business case.

CHAMPS

The five qualification answers: Challenge, Authority, Money, Priority and Stack / Storage.

CHAMPS — Authority

The contact’s role and influence in the buying decision, including access to those who approve the purchase.

Example “The architect recommends the design; the infrastructure director approves the budget.”

CHAMPS — Challenge

The customer’s initial problem or unmet need, supported by customer evidence.

Example “Researchers wait overnight for datasets to be ready for analysis.”

CHAMPS — Money

Evidence of the customer’s funding, funding access and ability to pay for the project.

Example “Estimated budget: $400,000. Funding approval depends on acceptance of the business case.”

CHAMPS — Priority

The urgency and business importance of solving the customer’s problem, including the consequence of delay.

Example “The platform is needed before the January research program; delay would postpone researcher onboarding.”

CHAMPS — Stack / Storage

The customer’s current technical environment, workloads and relevant storage requirements.

Example “Shared storage supports ingest and model training; the reported ingest bottleneck needs validation.”

Checkpointing

Saving training state so a job can resume after an interruption. Checkpoint size, write time and restore time affect storage requirements and productive training time.

Close Date

The expected close date while open, confirmed as the decision date at closure and locked at Closed won.

Closed lost

The outcome recording the customer’s decision not to buy, with the reason, competitive outcome, learning and follow-up.

Commit

The sales forecast category for Stages 5–6.

Competitor Detail (if Other)

The company name required whenever Other is selected in Competitors or Competitor Lost To.

Competitor Lost To

The competitive outcome at loss. Optional for Opportunity Misqualified, Project Cancelled / Budget Lost and No Decision / Went Dark; required for other reasons. Record only a supported outcome.

Example The project is cancelled without a confirmed vendor selection. Competitor Lost To can remain blank; the loss reason and explanation are still recorded.

Competitors and status quo

The alternatives the customer is considering, including other vendors and keeping the current approach.

Contact for Win/Loss Interview

The customer Contact the Win/Loss team may approach when interview eligibility is Yes.

Conversion Contact

The designated originating Contact resolved and associated with the opportunity for conversion tracking.

Conversion tracking

MQL and AQL are distinct Contact qualification paths. Opportunity creation records SAL; first qualified pipeline entry records SQL, including valid skips past Discovery.

Coordinating deployment Case

The coordinating deployment Case starts when OM books the deal at Stage 7. It holds the delivery plan, workstreams and responsibilities through Stage 8 and after the Opportunity ends.

Cost per token

The cost of processing or generating tokens under a defined workload and cost scope, often expressed per million tokens. Provider delivery cost and the customer’s billed price are different measures.

CSP — Cloud Service Provider

A company that delivers computing, storage, networking or software services over a network. NeoClouds are a specialized type of CSP.

Customer meetings and follow-up

Meeting Tasks connect a customer conversation to its owner, date, account or deal, outcome and next actions. Synergy can create follow-up Tasks by voice; calendar sync is still outstanding.

Data ingest

Bringing data from its sources into a platform so it can be stored, prepared and used. Ingest rate and data format affect how quickly new data becomes available.

Data locality

How close data is to the compute that uses it, physically or logically. Placement affects transfer time, network traffic and the need to copy datasets.

Data pipeline

The sequence that collects, moves, prepares and delivers data to a workload. A slow step can limit the entire workflow even when GPUs are available.

Data staging

Placing the required dataset where a workload can access it before processing begins. Staging time can delay the start of a training or analysis job.

DDN Data Intelligence Platform

DDN’s data infrastructure platform, including EXAScaler and Infinia, for storing, accessing and managing data across AI and HPC workflows.

Deal context and technical evidence

The customer, solution and business-need summary, supported by links to current technical documentation.

Deal registration

A partner registration linked to a deal. It can apply regardless of who originated the opportunity and does not automatically determine Opportunity Source.

Example A seller-originated deal can retain Direct source when a transacting partner later registers it.

Deal Team Loss Report

The restricted report due within two business days for a qualifying loss.

Deal Team Win Report

The additional win report required above $2M.

Decision Criteria

The requirements the customer uses to evaluate and compare solutions.

Example The solution must meet the agreed workload performance, usable capacity and integration requirements.

Decision Process

The steps, participants and timing through which the customer selects a solution.

Example Technical review → director’s value review → steering committee selection.

Delivery Notes

Instructions and context for shipping, receiving and deployment.

Example “Receiving dock open weekdays 08:00–16:00. Confirm site access with the receiving contact before dispatch.”

Delivery-led Opportunity completion

Completion of the Opportunity at Stage 8 when the full in-scope order is verified as delivered on site. Financial invoicing, deployment and value assessment are separate events.

Deployment & value

The delivery and validation of the customer’s agreed outcome, managed on the coordinating Case. It continues after Stage 8 rather than adding an Opportunity stage.

Deployment Case Link

The Opportunity link to the coordinating PS deployment Case started at Stage 7 booking; it remains available through Stage 8 and subsequent Case work.

Example OM books the deal at Stage 7, and the same transition creates or links one coordinating Case for GSS.

Description

The concise opportunity narrative covering customer context, intended solution and business need, required in Stage 2.

Example “The lab needs to reduce dataset preparation delays for a new research program using a validated storage configuration.”

Economic Buyer

The person who controls the budget for the purchase.

Enterprise AI

AI applied to an organization’s business workflows and data, with the security, governance and operational controls needed for production use.

Estimated Shipping Date

The date the order is expected to ship.

EXAScaler

DDN’s Lustre-based parallel file system solution for high-throughput AI and HPC workloads. It provides shared file access for many compute clients working at scale.

File storage

Storage accessed as named files in directories through a file system. Applications use file operations to open, read, write and manage data.

Final Agreements Received

Confirmation that the final agreements needed to complete procurement have been received.

Fine-tuning

Additional training of an existing model on selected data to adapt it to a task, domain or behavior. It changes model parameters.

Forecast Category (Sales)

The sales forecast classification derived from the Opportunity stage: Pre-pipeline, Pipeline, Upside, Commit, Won or Loss.

GPU capacity occupancy

The share of a provider’s available GPU capacity allocated or committed to customers over a defined period. This commercial measure is separate from GPU compute utilization and CUDA SM occupancy.

Example If 80 of 100 comparable GPUs are customer-allocated for the same period, allocation-based capacity occupancy is 80%.

GPU cluster

A connected group of GPU servers that can run coordinated workloads. Cluster performance depends on compute, networking, storage and software working together.

GPU memory utilization

A GPU memory metric whose meaning depends on the tool: it may describe memory activity or the share of memory capacity occupied. State the metric explicitly; neither is the same as compute utilization.

GPU starvation

A situation in which GPUs wait for input data instead of progressing the workload. Storage, networking or data preparation may cause the delay; the bottleneck needs measurement.

GPU usage / consumption

The amount of GPU resource used or allocated over a period, often measured in GPU-hours. Specify whether the figure means allocated time, billed consumption or active computation.

Example Eight GPUs allocated for ten hours equal 80 allocated GPU-hours, even if the workload leaves them idle part of the time.

GPU utilization

A measure of GPU activity over time. NVIDIA’s common utilization metric reports the share of sampled time with a GPU kernel running; a high reading alone does not prove useful work or peak efficiency.

Example A GPU can be allocated to a paying customer all day while spending part of that day waiting for data.

GPU — Graphics Processing Unit

A processor designed to perform many calculations in parallel. GPUs accelerate AI training, inference and other compute-intensive workloads.

GPU-hour

One GPU allocated or used for one hour, under a stated measurement or billing definition. GPU model, service configuration and billing terms must be comparable when discussing price per GPU-hour.

GPUaaS — GPU as a Service

Access to a provider’s GPUs as a service, rather than purchasing and operating the hardware. The offer may include storage, networking and management.

HPC — High-Performance Computing

Using powerful, often interconnected computers to solve demanding problems such as simulation, modeling and scientific analysis. HPC and AI can share data infrastructure while having different workload needs.

Hyperscaler

A provider operating cloud infrastructure at very large scale, usually with a broad portfolio of services and regions. AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud are common examples.

Identify Pain

The customer’s problem and its validated impact on people, operations or business results. It develops the initial CHAMPS Challenge into a clear reason to act.

Example “Staging delays cause missed processing windows and repeated rescheduling each week.”

In Progress — Technical Outcome

Technical evaluation or customer preference is not yet resolved. Record blockers and next actions; this state cannot pass the Stage 3 exit gate.

Incumbent Vendor(s)

The vendors the customer currently uses, or Not Applicable if there are none.

Indirect Route Type

The specific purchasing motion used when Route to Market is Indirect.

Inference

Running a trained model on new inputs to produce outputs, such as answers, predictions or generated content.

Infinia

DDN’s software-defined data platform with multi-tenancy, metadata capabilities and multi-protocol access. It supports data-intensive workflows including AI data preparation, analytics and inference.

Invoice Reference / Invoice Date

Evidence recorded and reconciled when the financial invoice is issued.

IOPS — Input/Output Operations per Second

The number of storage read or write operations completed each second. Interpret IOPS alongside operation size, access pattern and latency.

IPG Finalized

The IPG disposition: Completed or explicitly Not Required.

IPG Process

The process for gathering, finalizing and submitting the IPG package during procurement and carrying it into the order and deployment handoff.

Joint

For non-Partner-only opportunities, classification when the first qualifying partner event occurs in Stages 3–6.

Example The first qualifying partner event occurs in Stage 4.

Latency

The time taken to complete a defined operation or return a response. State the operation and measurement, such as average or 99th-percentile read latency.

Lead time and logistics

Planning that connects shipping, customer receipt and installation, including the dates, responsibilities and dependencies for each.

LLM — Large Language Model

An AI model trained on large amounts of data to understand and generate language. Its size and usage pattern affect memory, compute and data requirements.

Loss

The sales forecast category for Closed lost.

Loss Reason

The specific reason the customer did not buy.

Loss Reason Details

The explanation and evidence behind the loss, including the customer’s decision, relevant factors and lessons.

Lost deal review

An assessment of why the customer did not buy, what the team learned and what follow-up is appropriate.

Lost — Next Step Recommendation

The recommended follow-up after the opportunity is lost.

Example “Reconnect after the customer’s next funding review in January.”

Lustre

An open-source parallel file system designed for large-scale, high-performance computing. DDN EXAScaler builds on Lustre.

Meeting Task

A Task that records a customer meeting’s purpose, owner, scheduled date, customer/deal relationship and outcome. Follow-up actions are separate owned Tasks.

Metadata

Information describing data, such as its name, size, owner, timestamps or tags. Metadata performance matters when applications discover or manage large numbers of files or objects.

Model loading

Moving model weights from storage into the memory needed to run the model. Loading time can affect service startup, recovery and switching between models.

MQL — Marketing Qualified Lead

The Contact qualification milestone supplied by Marketing’s automation platform.

Multi-tenancy

Serving multiple tenants on shared infrastructure while keeping their data, access and resource controls separate. Isolation and predictable performance matter when different customers share the platform.

NCP — NVIDIA Cloud Partner

An AI cloud provider in the NVIDIA Partner Network that delivers services built on NVIDIA’s accelerated computing platform. It is a partner designation, not a synonym for every GPU cloud provider.

NeoCloud

A cloud provider focused on GPU computing and AI workloads such as training and inference. NeoCloud describes the business model; NCP describes a relationship with NVIDIA. A company can be both.

Next Step

A dated customer-facing action maintained with expected Close Date in open Stages 1–6.

Example “Friday: AE and director review the business case and confirm the procurement sequence.”

Next Steps and Close Date review

The weekly confirmation of the next customer action and expected Close Date for open opportunities in Stages 1–6. The SE reviews its technical action separately.

NVIDIA Engagement

NVIDIA’s involvement in an opportunity, recorded through its account-specific engagement plan and the Opportunity’s engagement summary and status.

NVIDIA Engagement Status

The Opportunity-level status of NVIDIA’s engagement, separate from the association’s Active/Inactive status.

NVIDIA-Certified Storage

NVIDIA’s program for validating storage against specified AI infrastructure requirements. Certification applies to the tested product and configuration scope, not automatically to every offering from a vendor.

Object storage

Storage that organizes data as objects with identifiers and metadata, typically accessed through an API. It differs from accessing files through a mounted directory hierarchy.

Off-taker

The customer buying GPU capacity or AI services from a NeoCloud or NVIDIA Cloud Partner (NCP). The provider may buy DDN infrastructure to serve that customer; the off-taker is not necessarily DDN’s direct buyer.

Example An AI company reserves a GPU cluster from a NeoCloud. The AI company is the off-taker; the NeoCloud operates the service.

OK to Interview (Win/Loss)?

Whether the Win/Loss team may approach a customer Contact. Required for applicable New / Existing / Renewal outcomes, except Stage 0 Opportunity Misqualified. Yes needs a Contact; No needs a cannot-approach reason.

Operational Value

The measurable improvement the solution is expected to deliver to the customer’s operations, expressed as a baseline, target and measurement approach.

Example “Reduce dataset staging from 8 hours to a 2-hour target, measured on the same representative dataset.”

Opportunity Contact Roles

The roles assigned to customer Contacts involved in the opportunity’s buying decision.

Opportunity Conversion Lifecycle

The opportunity’s last conversion milestone: SAL at creation or SQL at first qualified pipeline entry, including valid skips. It is separate from the numbered sales stage.

Opportunity creation

The capture of a new opportunity, its source, originating Contact and core deal information. Inbound intake starts in Validate; seller-self-qualified opportunities may start in Discovery.

Opportunity Name

The name identifying the customer opportunity or project.

Example “Research Lab — AI Training Storage Expansion”

Opportunity Owner / Assigned AE

The seller accountable for the opportunity.

Opportunity Source

Opportunity Source records the verified origin of a deal: Direct, BDR, Marketing or Partner. Its sourcing reference identifies the originating user, campaign or partner.

Example A BDR originates the deal and an AE takes ownership. The source remains BDR, with the originating BDR linked as Sourcing User.

Opportunity Type

The sales motion: New Business, Existing Business, Renewal, Internal Transfer or Non-Revenue.

Order Review Status

The current status of Order Management’s review and processing of the order.

Paper Process

The legal, procurement, signature and purchase-order steps required to complete the transaction.

Example Legal review → vendor onboarding → agreement signature → PO issuance.

Parallel file system

A file system that distributes data and I/O across multiple storage resources so many clients can access shared data concurrently at scale.

Partner Admin Contact

The partner’s administrative point of contact on its Account. This relationship does not grant system or approval permissions.

Partner Contribution

The classification of partner involvement based on channel ownership at creation and the earliest qualifying partner event. Automated credit freezes at booking.

Partner contribution override

A management-approved correction to partner-contribution reporting, supported by a recorded reason.

Partner Technical Lead

The internal technical coverage owner on a partner Account, brought into relevant opportunities through active partner associations.

Partner-Led

For non-Partner-only opportunities, classification based on the earliest qualifying partner event before Stage 3.

Example The first qualifying partner event occurs in Stage 2.

Partner-only

Contribution classification for an opportunity created and owned solely by the partner/channel.

Pipeline

The sales forecast category for Stages 1–2.

Pipeline Date

The first valid entry into any numbered Stage 1–8, including allowed direct Stage 1 creation. It differs from Created Date and does not reset on regression or re-entry.

Example A permitted 0 → 4 move sets Pipeline Date equal to Stage 4 Date; Stage 1 Date stays blank until that stage is actually visited.

Pipeline Entry Amount

The Amount and currency context captured at first valid entry into any numbered Stage 1–8. It stays fixed for pipeline-generation reporting while current Amount may change.

Example A deal enters Stage 1 at $300,000 and is later quoted at $400,000. First-entry credit remains $300,000; current Amount reflects the later quote.

PO Secured / PO Attachment

Confirmation that the purchase order has been received, with the supporting PO document attached.

POC Required?

The Yes/No decision on whether a proof of concept is needed for the opportunity.

POC Status

The current state of the proof of concept. A required POC must be Cancelled or Completed before Stage 3.

POC — Proof of concept

A proof of concept: a scoped evaluation used to test technical requirements. A required POC must be Cancelled or Completed before Stage 3; completion alone does not establish a Technical Win.

Pre-pipeline

The sales forecast category for creation/Stage 0.

Primary DDN Partner Account Manager

The internal commercial owner of a partner relationship, maintained on the partner Account and used for scoped opportunity-team assignment.

Primary Quote

The selected CPQ quote that supplies the opportunity’s quoted Amount and Products.

Primary SE

The Sales Engineering lead responsible for technical discovery and solution work.

Procurement evidence

The documents and confirmations needed to complete procurement, including the PO, final agreements, IPG disposition and applicable delivery information.

Product enhancements

Requests for needed product capabilities, documented in Cases linked to the opportunity. A request is not a delivery commitment.

QoS — Quality of Service

Controls or service targets that govern how resources and performance are shared. QoS helps prevent one tenant or workload from overwhelming others.

Quote Purpose

The purpose of the CPQ request: Budgetary or Best and Final.

Example Budgetary for a planning estimate; Best and Final for the final commercial proposal.

Quoting

CPQ turns scope and pricing into a commercial proposal. Controlled Budgetary quotes are available in Stages 0–1; Stages 2–5 support broader quoting, with technical and commercial gates for Best and Final.

RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Retrieving relevant information and supplying it to a generative model as context for an answer. RAG can use current enterprise information without retraining the model for every document update.

Example An assistant retrieves approved product documentation before answering a customer’s configuration question.

Reference architecture

A documented design showing how components should work together for a use case. Alignment to a reference architecture does not by itself prove certification or the customer’s measured performance.

Requested Delivery Date

The date the customer wants the order to arrive.

Requested Install Date

The date the customer wants installation to take place.

Resources Engaged

The internal account-team and cross-functional contributors to the win, recorded for wins of $1M or more.

RevOps Notes

Restricted open-text operational context maintained by RevOps on the Account.

ROI — Return on Investment

Net benefit divided by investment cost over a defined period, usually expressed as a percentage. Use a consistent cost basis and customer-validated benefit assumptions.

Example With $150,000 in benefits and $100,000 in total investment costs over the same period, ROI is 50%.

Route to Market

How the sale will transact: Direct or Indirect. Indirect requires an applicable Indirect Route Type.

Example A Marketing-origin opportunity transacts through a reseller: source is Marketing; route is Indirect.

S3-compatible

Supporting Amazon S3-style APIs for object access. Compatibility does not mean the service runs on AWS or supports every S3 feature.

SAL acceptance / rejection

The assigned seller’s decision to accept or reject an opportunity handoff. This decision is separate from the SAL milestone recorded at opportunity creation.

SAL — Sales Accepted Lead

The conversion milestone recorded when an opportunity is created. Despite its name, SAL does not itself confirm the assigned seller’s acceptance; that is a separate decision.

Sales Order Reference

The reference connecting the opportunity to its booked sales order.

SE Next Step

The Sales Engineering technical next action, reviewed separately from the AE’s customer/commercial next step.

Example “Wednesday: SE and architect validate the migration requirement.”

Seller-self-qualified entry

Direct creation of a seller-qualified opportunity in Stage 1 by its assigned seller, recording auto-accepted SAL and SQL. CHAMPS is completed before Stage 2.

SharePoint Link

Links to the technical documentation supporting the opportunity.

SLA — Service-Level Agreement

An agreement defining service commitments, how they are measured and what happens if they are missed. Availability, performance and support response commitments should be read in their specific contract context.

Sourcing Campaign

The campaign credited with originating a Marketing opportunity.

Sourcing Partner

The partner Account that actually originated an opportunity with Source = Partner. Registration, transaction and later contribution are separate relationships.

Sourcing User

The person credited with originating a Direct or BDR opportunity.

Sovereign AI

AI developed and operated with control over infrastructure, data, models and operations aligned to a nation’s or organization’s requirements. Data location is one part of that control.

SQL — Sales Qualified Lead

The sales qualification milestone recorded on first valid entry into Stages 1–8 after the applicable acceptance and qualification gates pass. It includes permitted skips and seller-self-qualified direct Stage 1 creation.

Example A valid Stage 0 → 4 transition records SQL and Pipeline Date, plus Stage 4 Date. Stage 1 Date stays blank because Discovery was not visited.

Stage 0 Date

The system-maintained date and time of first actual entry to Stage 0. Skipped stages stay blank and re-entry does not reset the value. Use transition history to measure time across repeated visits.

Stage 0 — Validate

The stage for reviewing an inbound handoff, assigning the seller and completing acceptance and CHAMPS qualification before Discovery.

Stage 1 Date

The system-maintained date and time of first actual entry to Stage 1. Skipped stages stay blank and re-entry does not reset the value. Use transition history to measure time across repeated visits.

Stage 1 — Discovery

The stage for establishing business fit, use case, buying roles and route to market. Entry records SQL; discovery is completed before Solution design.

Stage 2 Date

The system-maintained date and time of first actual entry to Stage 2. Skipped stages stay blank and re-entry does not reset the value. Use transition history to measure time across repeated visits.

Stage 2 — Solution design

The stage for evaluating technical fit through candidate configurations, supported by pain, criteria, competitor and champion evidence.

Stage 3 Date

The system-maintained date and time of first actual entry to Stage 3. Skipped stages stay blank and re-entry does not reset the value. Use transition history to measure time across repeated visits.

Stage 3 — Buyer alignment

The stage for agreeing one configuration and aligning the technical outcome, operational value, Economic Buyer and Decision Process.

Stage 4 Date

The system-maintained date and time of first actual entry to Stage 4. Skipped stages stay blank and re-entry does not reset the value. Use transition history to measure time across repeated visits.

Stage 4 — Proposal / negotiate

The stage for aligning the commercial proposal, quantified Business Value, Primary Quote and Paper Process.

Stage 5 Date

The system-maintained date and time of first actual entry to Stage 5. Skipped stages stay blank and re-entry does not reset the value. Use transition history to measure time across repeated visits.

Stage 5 — Procurement

The stage for completing the PO, final agreements, IPG disposition and applicable shipping and delivery information.

Stage 6 Date

The system-maintained date and time of first actual entry to Stage 6. Skipped stages stay blank and re-entry does not reset the value. Use transition history to measure time across repeated visits.

Stage 6 — Won decision

The customer’s won decision, followed by order review and deployment planning. The opportunity remains Commit until booking is confirmed.

Stage 7 — Closed won

The OM-confirmed booked order, with locked booked facts and Close Date. Fulfillment and deployment coordination continue.

Stage 8 — Invoice

The final Opportunity stage, reached at verified delivery of the complete in-scope order. Invoice evidence is captured when issued; deployment and value work continue on the Case.

Synergy voice follow-up

The Synergy voice interface for creating meeting follow-up Tasks. Review owner, due date and customer/deal links before saving. Calendar sync remains outstanding.

Target Value Realization Date

The planned date for confirming that the customer has achieved the agreed value.

TCO — Total Cost of Ownership

The full cost of acquiring and operating a solution over a stated period, including relevant hardware, software, support, staffing, power and facilities costs.

Technical Deal Risk

A technical issue or uncertainty that could affect the opportunity, recorded with its category, severity, explanation and linked issue.

Example “Migration dependency may delay launch. Category: Migration. Level: High. Linked issue contains the validation plan.”

Technical Deal Risk Category

The type of technical risk: Parts, Software, System, Expertise, Timeline, Integration, Migration, Engineering or Support.

Technical fit

Evidence that the proposed solution meets the customer’s requirements. Customer preference for DDN is additionally needed for Technical Win.

Technical Level of Risk

The severity or resolution of a technical risk: High, Medium, Low or Resolved.

Technical Loss

The Technical Outcome indicating a loss in technical evaluation.

Technical Outcome

Technical Outcome records In Progress, Technical Win or Technical Loss. A win means the customer prefers DDN against the agreed criteria, supported by SE evidence and the selected configuration, subject to commercial agreement.

Technical Value

How the proposed solution meets the customer’s technical requirements, including the configuration and evidence supporting its fit.

Technical Win

Customer-confirmed preference for DDN against agreed decision criteria, supported by SE evidence and the selected configuration, subject to commercial agreement.

Example The customer confirms DDN is its preferred technical solution after reviewing the agreed criteria and configuration; commercial agreement is still pending.

Tenant

A customer or organizational unit with its own access, resources and policies within a shared platform. A tenant may have many users.

Throughput

The amount of data successfully transferred per unit of time, often MB/s or GB/s. Sustained workload throughput can differ from a component’s advertised maximum.

Time to insight

The elapsed time from starting a data workflow to obtaining a usable result or decision. It can include data preparation, staging, processing and analysis.

Time to train

The elapsed time needed to train a model to an agreed quality target. Compare the same model, data and quality threshold when evaluating infrastructure improvements.

Time to Value

The number of days from first verified on-site delivery to confirmed value achievement. It is calculated on the coordinating Case only when Value Achieved? is Yes.

Example Delivery verified October 10; value confirmed October 24: Time to Value is 14 days.

Timing Urgency

The CPQ timing classification, Standard by default or Urgent with an Urgency Reason.

Example “Urgent: proposal needed before the customer’s Friday funding-approval meeting.”

Token

A unit of content processed by an AI model, such as a word fragment or punctuation mark in text. Token counts depend on the model’s tokenizer; one token is not always one word.

Tokens per second

The rate at which a model or service processes or generates tokens. State whether it is input or output throughput and whether the measurement is per user, per GPU or across the service.

Transacting Partner

The Transacting Partner is the primary partner through which the commercial transaction is conducted. It can differ from the originating partner and the physical ship-to destination.

TTFT — Time to First Token

The time from submitting a request until the first output token arrives. It describes the initial wait, not the time needed to finish the response.

Upside

The sales forecast category for Stages 3–4.

Usable capacity

Storage capacity available for customer data after protection and system overheads. Raw drive capacity and usable capacity are different numbers.

Use Case

The activity or workflow the customer intends to support with the solution, and the outcome they want to achieve.

Example AI model training that requires faster dataset preparation between experiments.

Value Achieved?

The Case’s value-assessment outcome: Yes means achieved and confirmed; No means assessed and not achieved; blank means the assessment is pending.

Value Outcome Confirmed Date

The date of the final Yes/No assessment of customer value.

Value realization

Confirmation that the customer achieved the agreed operational and business outcomes, supported by measured results and customer evidence.

Value Realization Context

The measured results and customer evidence supporting the value assessment, or the gap and agreed action if value was not achieved.

Example “Customer measured 2-hour staging against the original 8-hour baseline and confirmed the agreed target was achieved.”

Value Realization Plan

The Case-based plan for validating the sales-defined operational and business outcomes, begun when the deployment Case starts at Stage 7.

Value Tracking Start Date

The first verified on-site delivery date used as the starting point for measuring Time to Value.

Weekly review freshness

How recently the AE confirmed the next customer action and expected Close Date, or the SE confirmed its technical next action.

Win wire

A manager-reviewed summary of why a deal was won, the customer value and the contributors. Above $1M, it is submitted to Enablement / #win-wire within two business days.

Win/Loss interview eligibility

Whether the Win/Loss team may approach a customer for feedback. Yes requires a Salesforce Contact; No requires a reason the team cannot approach.

WL Reason Cannot Approach

The reason the Win/Loss team cannot approach the customer for feedback when interview eligibility is No.

Won

The sales forecast category for Stages 7–8.

Won decision

The customer’s decision to buy, recorded before Order Management confirms the booked order. The sales forecast remains Commit.

Won Reason

The specific reason the customer selected the solution, captured on entry to Stage 6.
