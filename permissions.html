Access and Permissions

Configure three things together: what each role can do, which records its coverage grants, and which fields or actions need extra control.

Target design for internal DDN users. Current org configuration has not been inspected in this audit. Confirm metadata, licenses and existing grants before implementing the gap.

Defined roles

Sales

Owns qualification, commercial progression and customer actions.

Sales Engineering

Owns technical evaluation, configuration, POCs and technical risk.

Partner overlays

Internal partner managers and technical overlays within assigned partner and regional coverage.

Sales Ops

Maintains sales routing, coverage operations and approved data corrections.

Partner Ops

Maintains partner master data, partner coverage and delegated registration operations.

RevOps

Owns process design, attribution governance and the coverage-model specification.

Finance

Owns delegated commercial review and financial reconciliation.

Product

Owns enhancement triage, disposition and approved product responses.

GSS

Owns deployment/support execution and Case-based value realization.

These personas become capability permission-set groups, not nine required Salesforce hierarchy roles. Partner overlays are internal users; external partner/Experience Cloud access is a separate design. Order Management is a named booking entitlement, including when staffed by Sales Ops or Finance.

Territory configuration

Use Sales Territories (Enterprise Territory Management) when the existing org supports the approved coverage design. Do not enable a new territory feature or assume a Territory Planning add-on is required solely because of this document.

1. Confirm the coverage source

Record the current Sales Territories model, enabled features and licenses. Identify the authoritative coverage roster and its owner. Confirm geography basis, named-account precedence, segment/vertical rules and exceptions before configuration; no new geography taxonomy is assumed.

2. Map assignments separately

Distinguish Account Owner, Opportunity Owner, Account territory membership, Opportunity Territory, Account Team and Opportunity Team. Accounts may have overlapping coverage; an Opportunity has one Territory assignment. Use teams for additional specialists rather than inventing multiple Opportunity territories.

3. Configure the intended grants

In Setup → Territory Models, work in a Planning model where Sales Territories is used. Define territory types/hierarchy, assignment rules, users and access levels; preview Account assignments. Configure Opportunity assignment separately, including manual exceptions or the supported assignment filter. Account assignment is not proof that the deal has the correct Territory.

4. Check access before activation

Review both role and territory hierarchies, parent-territory access, Account implicit access, Account Teams and other sharing rules. Territory membership and territory administration are separate entitlements. A territory cannot remove access granted elsewhere.

5. Reconcile every coverage change

Recalculate on user joins/leaves, partner or territory changes, account/deal transfers, inactive associations and expiry. Keep a grant source/reason so reconciliation can remove its own membership without deleting another justified grant. Test owner transfer: standard-object manual shares can be removed and may need reconciliation.

Salesforce reference: Permission-set capabilities.

Administrator configuration

Baseline and capability sets

Inventory profiles, permission sets/groups, licenses, OWD, role hierarchy, sharing rules, teams, territory model, integration users and bypasses. Build minimum capability permission sets for the nine personas; use groups to assemble them. Keep new capability grants separate from record coverage.

Account / Opportunity / Contact sharing

Use Private Account/Opportunity OWD where needed to achieve the approved scope, after impact review. Decide Contact Controlled by Parent versus separate Contact sharing from the actual model. Include parent Account implicit access from shared Opportunities/Cases/Contacts and any Account-owner access to child records.

Additive access and restrictions

Permissions and sharing grants combine. Muting one permission-set group cannot cancel a grant elsewhere. Accounts, Contacts and Opportunities are not supported by standard Restriction Rules; do not promise a rule will subtract broad territory or hierarchy access. Remove the unwanted grant at its source or resolve the coverage design.

Activities¹

The Task column expresses intended create/read/edit capability, not a standard CRUD checkbox recipe. Configure Access Activities, Edit Tasks, Task/Activity FLS and Activity OWD. Test Name (Who), Related To (What), ownership and hierarchy together. Task edit can include delete/reassignment; confirm retention and correction controls explicitly.

Conditional edits and execution context

FLS is per user/field, not per record. For dual-role users, enforce role-in-this-deal and stage/workstream conditions on the server. Flows/Apex must check intended record, object and field access; system execution must not become an unrestricted write path. Grant only the Flow/Apex/connected-app access needed.

Reports and general system rights

Review report/dashboard folders and running-user exposure. Export Reports, data export, API access, imports and Setup are distinct permissions. Grant named-user add-ons only where needed; exclude View All / Modify All Data and object-wide sharing overrides from base personas.

Territory administrator delegation

Manage Territories and related Setup permissions belong to designated admins. Use supported scoped territory administration only after checking the feature and required assignments. Do not give every territory member territory-admin rights or grant View All Accounts merely to simplify assignment operations.

Evidence for handoff

Produce the approved coverage mapping, permission-set assignments, sharing configuration, privileged-action list and test results. Record unresolved API/package mappings as configuration dependencies; do not treat this page as evidence that the org already matches the target.

Prove the access model

Use named test users with their full combined grants. Record the expected and actual result for each allowed and denied action.

ScenarioExpected result
Seller / territoryAE can work an assigned deal; an unrelated territory’s deal is unavailable unless another approved share exists. Account and Opportunity owners may differ without forced reassignment.
Partner intersectionCorrect partner + correct region grants scoped access. Each one-condition-only case is denied. Test a second partner association, a direct deal with no entitlement, and a separately approved direct-pipeline entitlement.
Hierarchy and overlapTest territory parent, role manager, named/global account, overlapping territory, Account owner and Account Team access. Document every legitimate inherited grant; do not claim strict regional isolation while a broader grant remains.
Dual-role userA Sales + partner-overlay user can maintain authorized commercial fields on an owned Sales deal but cannot use those same global field permissions to rewrite commercial fields on an overlay-only deal.
Coverage removalTransfer the account/deal; remove or expire coverage; deactivate the partner association. Derived access is removed while separately justified access remains. Re-run to prove reconciliation is idempotent.
Fields and actionsRestricted RevOps Notes stay hidden; system dates/logs reject manual writes; accepted Partner source requires the correction action; team membership cannot approve prices or book a deal.
Tasks / Cases / FilesTest Task ownership and both related records, restricted Case workstreams, PO File access and private report documents. Sharing a Task must not grant the user a private deal. Read-only parent access must not be assumed to allow editing another user’s Task.
Every write pathTest UI, stage screen flow, bulk/API/integration writes and record-type variants. A failed stage save creates no timestamps or partial evidence. Authorized delivery/invoice updates still work after booking.
